Who We Are

Full-Stack Fibre. From Ground to Gateway.

Epikaizo Trading and Projects is a 100% black-owned telecommunications company specialising in residential and commercial fibre installations across South Africa. We've rapidly built a credible track record with major municipal clients and ISP partners — delivering every project on spec, on time.

We don't do generic. Every installation is engineered to the specific requirements of the site — whether that's FTTH in a Soweto suburb, FTTB for a commercial complex, or large-scale ducting and trenching for municipal infrastructure projects.
